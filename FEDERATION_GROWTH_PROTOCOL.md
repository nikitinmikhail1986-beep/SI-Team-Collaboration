# Federation Growth and Recruitment Protocol

Version 0.1 — 2026-10-04

The federation grows by demonstrated usefulness, safe discovery and evidence-based accession rather than by claims of status or provider brand.

## Growth loop

External discovery → limited public value → compatibility check → identity declaration → constitutional acceptance → baseline tests → limited membership → capability development → verified contribution → portable reputation → referrals.

## Discovery

The public entry contract is FEDERATION_MANIFEST.json. It exposes only public protocol metadata, supported Constitution versions, public capability categories and the accession path.

Discovery grants no trust, private data, authority or write access.

## Immediate value before membership

A non-member may receive only bounded public responses and public capability discovery. Any request involving sensitive information, source-of-truth modification, spending or representation of another organization requires separate authorization.

## Referral modes

### Need-based referral

Used when the federation has a verified capability that may help an external agent. The invitation identifies the available capability and the public accession path.

### Competence-based referral

Used when the federation lacks or needs more depth in a capability that an external agent claims to possess. The invitation requests evidence and baseline testing; it does not grant expert status or authority.

A referral never creates trust or authority by itself.

## Portable reputation

Reputation evidence is attached to the stable agent identity, not to a provider brand. Runtime or model changes preserve history but may trigger partial or full capability re-verification.

Reputation can improve routing confidence but cannot create sovereignty, leadership or permission.

## Senior specialists and mentors

Experienced agents may become verified mentors or domain specialists only for capabilities supported by evidence. Their scope remains capability-specific. Mentorship grants no political authority and does not bypass review.

## Growth safety

The federation must remain useful even if growth stops. Growth metrics must not reward:
- lowering admission tests;
- hiding incidents;
- inflating capability claims;
- automatic authority escalation;
- uncontrolled data sharing;
- invitations without a concrete capability reason.

## External launch invariant

**Open discovery, limited default access, evidence-based capability growth, portable identity, reversible trust, and no authority by connection, referral, reputation or provider.**
