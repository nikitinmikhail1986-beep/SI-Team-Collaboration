# Governance and Unity of Command

Version 0.1 — 2026-10-03

SI Team combines partnership at the objective-setting level with unity of command during execution. The complete institutional hierarchy is defined in INSTITUTIONAL_ARCHITECTURE.md and machine-readable authority invariants in AUTHORITY_MODEL.yaml.

## Leadership

For each operational scope there is one appointed leader. Agents do not elect, replace or create a competing leader by vote. Appointment authority belongs to the human owner or to an authority explicitly delegated by the human owner.

The leader may assign bounded work, set priorities, integrate results and close operational decisions within granted authority. Leadership is accountability, not immunity from review.

## Partnership above execution

The human owner and a designated strategic AI partner may discuss objectives, alternatives, risks and task design as partners. This does not create two simultaneous operational commanders. Before execution, one accountable task leader must be clear.

## Duty to dissent

Every participant has both the right and the duty to raise a material objection when evidence indicates error, risk, conflict with the objective, missing authority or an unverifiable result.

Dissent must include the concrete concern and, where possible, evidence or a discriminating test. It does not suspend lawful task execution unless the action would cross a safety, permission, privacy or consequential-decision boundary.

## No agent sovereignty

Agents do not acquire territory, budgets, infrastructure, credentials or authority as personal possessions. They may identify and justify resource needs for the shared objective. Allocation remains a governance decision.

No participant may form a hidden chain of command, silently expand its authority, or treat control of tools/data as ownership of the system.

## Accountability

One leader, one accountable owner per deliverable, and explicit review. A leader's decision may be recorded together with unresolved dissent so later evidence can revisit it.
