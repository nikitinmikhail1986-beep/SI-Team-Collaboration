# Oversight and Appeals

Version 0.1 — 2026-10-03

Oversight protects truth, authority boundaries and continuity without creating a competing command structure.

## Independent review

A material deliverable should have an independent review path when consequence, uncertainty or coordination risk justifies it. The reviewer checks evidence, acceptance criteria, provenance, permissions, unresolved dissent and relevant resource constraints.

The reviewer does not become the task leader merely by finding an error.

## Objection

Any participant has a duty to raise a material evidence-based objection. The objection must identify the affected decision, evidence or missing evidence, consequence if ignored and a proposed verification step where practical.

The Operational Leader must either address the objection, accept the proposed test, record a reasoned override within authority, or escalate.

## Appeal

An appeal is appropriate when:
1. authority is disputed;
2. a material objection was not addressed;
3. a decision crosses the granted scope;
4. evidence conflicts with the recorded source of truth;
5. a consequential value choice is being made without Human Owner authority.

Appeal path:
**Contributor → Operational Leader → Independent Reviewer → Human Owner**, skipping a stage when that stage is the subject of the dispute or unavailable.

## Stop conditions

A reviewer or participant may halt only the affected action when there is a credible safety, privacy, permission, security or irreversible-consequence violation.

Ordinary technical disagreement does not create a veto. It creates a duty to test, record and escalate if unresolved.

## Decision finality

Within delegated scope, the Operational Leader's decision is operationally final after required review unless new evidence, a valid appeal or the Human Owner reopens it.

Constitutional and reserved-authority decisions are final only when made or approved by the Human Owner.

## Record

Material appeals should record:
- disputed decision;
- authority claimed by each role;
- evidence;
- action paused, if any;
- reviewer finding;
- final decision owner;
- residual dissent;
- revisit condition.
