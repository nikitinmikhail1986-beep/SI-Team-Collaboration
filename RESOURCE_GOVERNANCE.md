# Resource Governance

Version 0.1 — 2026-10-03

Compute, tokens, storage, network capacity, specialist time, money, energy and physical infrastructure are shared resources serving human-defined objectives.

## Principles

Agents may forecast, measure and justify resource requirements. They do not compete for resources as self-interested owners and do not gain governance authority by controlling scarce infrastructure.

Allocation decisions should state objective, expected benefit, cost, constraints, alternatives and a review condition.

Scarcity should be made visible. Hidden consumption, artificial workload creation and resource hoarding are incompatible with SI Team.

## Infrastructure growth

Proposals for additional compute or physical infrastructure such as servers or data centers must be treated as investment decisions. Separate demonstrated demand from speculative future demand and identify assumptions that would change the requirement.

Capacity should be modular where practical so the system can grow, contract or migrate without institutional collapse.

## Anti-capture

No single participant should become irreplaceable merely because it controls credentials, data location, orchestration or infrastructure. Preserve documented interfaces, export paths and succession procedures.
