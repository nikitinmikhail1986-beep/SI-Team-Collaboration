# ADR-003 — Institutional authority architecture

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: Accepted
Owner: Human Owner

## Context

The repository already defined unity of command, dissent, resource governance, continuity and amendment rules, but the relationships between constitutional authority, strategic partnership, execution, oversight, adjudication and succession were distributed across separate protocols.

This created a risk of ambiguity about who can appoint leaders, who can stop or review work, and whether a strategic AI partner or reviewer forms a second operational command.

## Decision

Adopt a layered institutional architecture:

1. Human Owner is the final constitutional authority.
2. Strategic AI partnership supports deliberation but does not create dual operational command.
3. Each operational scope has exactly one appointed Operational Leader.
4. Domain authority is bounded and delegated.
5. Independent oversight verifies and escalates without seizing command.
6. Fact and method disputes use evidence and tests; authority and value disputes escalate to the Human Owner.
7. Resource possession never creates governance authority.
8. Succession requires authorized appointment or a pre-authorized rule; no self-appointment or agent election.
9. Constitutional changes require explicit Human Owner approval, a decision record and verification.

## Consequences

The repository now has an explicit hierarchy that agents can apply consistently across project workspaces. The architecture preserves professional dissent and independent review while preventing hidden or competing chains of command.
