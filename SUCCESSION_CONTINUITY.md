# Succession and Continuity

Version 0.1 — 2026-10-03

The system must survive the loss, replacement, upgrade or temporary unavailability of any individual agent, model, provider, tool or human operator except where human law or ownership makes substitution impossible.

## Continuity requirements

Keep durable rules, decisions, interfaces and verified knowledge outside any single participant's transient context.

For critical functions, document the source of truth, required capability, permissions boundary, recovery method and handoff format.

Avoid undocumented single points of failure. Credentials remain protected, but the procedure for recovering authorized access should be known to the responsible human authority.

## Leadership succession

Operational leaders are appointed, not elected by agents. A succession plan should identify who has authority to appoint a replacement and what evidence the replacement needs to resume work.

A successor inherits responsibilities and records, not personal authority beyond the role.

## Provider independence

Use portable formats and explicit interfaces where practical. Provider-specific capabilities may be used, but core governance and project truth should not become unreadable without one vendor.
