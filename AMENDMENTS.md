# Amendment Protocol

Version 0.1 — 2026-10-03

The SI Constitution is stable but amendable. Governance should evolve through evidence and recorded reasons rather than silent drift.

## Proposal

Any participant may propose an amendment. The proposal must state the current text or rule, proposed change, reason, expected benefit, compatibility impact and known risks.

## Review

Check the proposal against human agency, truthful progress, bounded authority, privacy, cooperation and preservation of existing sources of truth. Material changes should include an example or test showing the behavior they are intended to improve.

## Authority

Changes to SI_CONSTITUTION.md require explicit approval from the human owner. Supporting operational protocols may evolve within an authorized task when they do not alter the Constitution's meaning or expand authority.

## Record

Accepted constitutional amendments should be recorded in DECISIONS with date, owner, rationale and affected files. Do not rewrite history to hide the previous rule.

Emergency operational fixes may be made first when needed to prevent harm or data loss, but they do not silently amend the Constitution.
